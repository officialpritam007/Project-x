Long description:

> Stay close through one-to-one conversations in Liquid Chat.
>
> Send text, share photos, and record voice messages in a clean Liquid Glass interface. Reply to a message, react, star important messages, and find your conversations with search, favorites, archive and unread filters.
>
> Personalize your profile, manage contacts and privacy settings, and mute or block unwanted contact. Share Stories with a 24-hour expiry and manage Posts for your contacts. Report messages, Stories, Posts or users when you need help.
>
> Choose light or dark appearance and adjust motion and transparency preferences. Queued messages stay on your device for retry when connectivity returns.
>
> Messages use encrypted network connections, but are not end-to-end encrypted. Read the Privacy & User Policy in the app for storage, reporting and deletion details. Delivery depends on connectivity, device restrictions and provider availability.
>
> Developed by Pritam Pal.
